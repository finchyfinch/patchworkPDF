What's new in this version
Version 3.3 opens faster and uses less memory.
  • Faster start-up, especially when you double-click a PDF to open it.
  • Uses much less memory: up to around 700 MB less on PCs with many processor cores.
  • A smaller download: unused components have been removed.
  • Light or dark mode and your language are now remembered every time you start the app.
  • Opening a PDF just after closing the app no longer says it is already running.
  • Linux: Export to Word works again.
From version 3.2: the Tools panel on the right has been reorganised.

Its tools are now on three tabs, by what they do to your document:

  • Comment — sticky notes, text, highlights and shapes that sit on top of the page. Everything you add is listed under the tools.
  • Edit — change the document itself: edit its text, add text or an image, redact, watermark and crop.
  • Fill & Sign — add form fields and sign.

The comments list shows who added each item and when. You can reply, set a status such as Accepted or Rejected, and filter by type, author or status. Put your name at the top of the Comment tab.

Coming from a version before 3.1? There is no separate Editor any more: its tools are on the Edit tab, changes apply straight away, and Ctrl+Z undoes them.
